Every problem on the desk today was a small example of the same idea: an AI model can only be as good as the data underneath it. Multiplied across millions of records, catches like these are how data quality becomes — or avoids becoming — the bias, blind spots and generalisability failures you'll dig into properly in Week 2.

Ask where it came from. Data collected for one purpose (like billing) doesn't automatically suit another (like diagnosis).
Ask who's in it — and who isn't. A model is only as representative as its training data.
Ask how current it is. Data has a shelf life; stale entries produce stale — and sometimes unsafe — recommendations.
