None of this governance is bureaucracy for its own sake. UK GDPR, the Caldicott Principles and the Goldacre Review exist because health data is uniquely sensitive and uniquely powerful — the same data that can train a life-saving AI tool can also cause real harm if it's mishandled.

UK GDPR sets the legal baseline — lawful basis, data minimisation, breach reporting within 72 hours.
The Caldicott Principles (and every organisation's Caldicott Guardian) govern how confidential patient information is used and shared.
The Goldacre Review pushed for secure data environments — so research can happen without raw patient data ever leaving NHS control.
