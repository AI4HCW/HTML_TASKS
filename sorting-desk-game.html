Most AI tools are trained overwhelmingly on structured data because it's easiest to process. Unstructured data holds huge amounts of clinical nuance, but usually needs natural language processing to extract anything a model can use — and detail is always lost in that conversion.

Structured — sits neatly in fields: vitals, blood results, ICD codes, NEWS2 scores.
Unstructured — free text: clinical notes, radiology reports, dictations, letters.
Semi-structured — a mix: DICOM imaging, some EHR exports.
