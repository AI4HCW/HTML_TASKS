🔁 It isn't a straight line. What real-world performance evaluation turns up at stage 7 often sends the team all the way back to stage 1 — a new problem to solve, or data that needs a second look.
You've walked the whole lifecycle. Here's the thing to take away from it: you're not just a passive user waiting for the next alert. You're a safety mechanism embedded in the system at multiple stages — flagging when a problem's been framed wrong, questioning data that doesn't look like your patients, noticing when a tool's output doesn't match the bedside, and reporting it when something looks off after deployment.
Stage names follow the FDA's total product lifecycle model for AI/ML-based medical devices.
